🎯 Goal
Create a list-building experience that feels as personal and expressive as your homepage feed, not like a form submission. This UI should feel lightweight, inspiring, and fluid — a continuation of the social, trust-based experience Circles stands for.

🧱 OVERALL DESIGN PRINCIPLES
Principle	Implementation
🧭 Clarity	One h1 at the top (“✨ Let’s build your list”), no subtext redundancy, no tabs or stepper
✍️ Low Friction	Let users add just one dish/restaurant to start; form auto-saves and previews immediately
👁 Live Feedback	Real-time list preview using feed-style cards as items are added
🎨 Homepage-Consistent Aesthetic	Soft shadows, clean cards, emoji accents, inline actions, muted helper text
🔘 Expressive Sharing	Replace dropdowns with card-style radio buttons for where to share (👤 Profile, 🫶 Circle, 🌍 Public, 🔒 Private)
