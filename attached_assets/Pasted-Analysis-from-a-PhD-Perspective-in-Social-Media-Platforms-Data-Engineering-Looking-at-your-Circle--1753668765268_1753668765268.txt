Location Data Pipeline Gap: "Unknown location" indicates your Google Places integration isn't properly hydrating location data during the search-to-detail page flow.

Confidence Level Miscommunication: The "low" indicator likely refers to confidence level due to limited data, but this isn't clearly communicated to users, creating confusion about data quality.
