🏁 Sprint Kickoff: Bolt-On Polishes – Detailed UI Requirements

Please use `MVP_Roadmap.md` as your spec. This sprint we’re focusing on Phase 1 “Bolt-On Polishes,” but you must follow these UI requirements exactly.

---

1. AppHeader (`/components/ui/AppHeader.tsx`)
   - **Structure**:  
     ```jsx
     <header className="flex items-center justify-between bg-white p-4 shadow-sm">
       <button 
         onClick={goBack} 
         aria-label="Back" 
         className="p-2 rounded-full focus:outline-none focus:ring-2 focus:ring-offset-2 focus:ring-primary"
       >
         <ChevronLeftIcon className="h-6 w-6 text-foreground"/>
       </button>
       <div className="flex-1 text-center">
         <img src="/logo.svg" alt="Circles" className="h-6 mx-auto" />
         <span className="sr-only">Circles</span>
       </div>
       <h1 className="absolute left-1/2 transform -translate-x-1/2 text-lg font-semibold">Page Title</h1>
       <div className="w-10" /> {/* spacer for symmetry */}
     </header>
     ```
   - **Notes**:  
     - Logo: `/logo.svg` or fallback text “Circles” with `text-xl font-bold`.  
     - Back button: tap target ≥44×44 px, focus ring as above.

2. Skeleton & Empty States
   - **FeedCard Skeleton** (`/components/ui/SkeletonFeedCard.tsx`):
     ```jsx
     <div className="animate-pulse p-4 bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm space-y-2">
       <div className="h-4 bg-gray-200 rounded w-3/4"></div>

       <div className="h-48 bg-gray-200 rounded"></div>

     ```

3. Error & Offline Banners
   - **Inline Error** (e.g. in `Feed.tsx`):
     ```jsx
     {error && (
       <div className="bg-red-100 border border-red-300 text-red-800 px-4 py-2 rounded mb-4">
         <p>Couldn’t load content.</p>
         <Button variant="outline" onClick={retry}>Try Again</Button>
       </div>
     )}
     ```
   - **Offline Banner** (`/components/ui/OfflineBanner.tsx`):
     ```jsx
     <div className="fixed top-0 inset-x-0 bg-yellow-100 text-yellow-900 text-center py-2 z-50">
       You’re offline—some features may be unavailable.
     </div>
     ```

---

4. Safe-Area & Tab Highlight
   - **Bottom Nav Container** (`/components/ui/BottomNav.tsx`):
     ```jsx
     <nav className="fixed bottom-0 inset-x-0 bg-white pb-safe pt-2 shadow-inner flex justify-around">
       {tabs.map(tab => (
         <button
           key={tab.key}
           aria-label={tab.label}
           className={`flex flex-col items-center ${
             activeTab === tab.key ? 'text-primary' : 'text-muted-foreground'
           }`}
         >
           <tab.Icon className="h-6 w-6"/>
           <span className="text-xs">{tab.label}</span>
         </button>
       ))}
     </nav>
     ```
   - **Floating Create Button** (`/components/ui/FloatingCreateButton.tsx`) should also use `bottom-safe` and sit above this nav.

---

5. Accessibility Quick Wins
   - **Tap Targets**: Validate every `<button>` has `min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px]` (use Tailwind utilities).  
   - **ARIA Labels**: Every icon-only button must have `aria-label="..."`.  
   - **Focus Styles**: Ensure `focus:outline-none focus:ring-2 focus:ring-offset-2 focus:ring-primary` is applied to all interactive elements.  
   - **Keyboard**: Popovers and modals must trap focus and close on `Esc`.
