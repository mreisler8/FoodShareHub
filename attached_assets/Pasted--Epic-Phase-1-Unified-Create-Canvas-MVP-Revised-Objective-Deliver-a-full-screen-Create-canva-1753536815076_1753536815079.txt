🧩 Epic: Phase 1 Unified Create Canvas MVP (Revised)
Objective: Deliver a full-screen “Create” canvas with List Starter and Food Moment modes, and wire those posts into the Home Feed, Profile, Discovery, and Follow features—drawing on best practices from Instagram, Pinterest, and Letterboxd.

📋 User Stories & Impact
1. List Starter
(As before: create title + one item in two taps.)

Propagation:

Home Feed: New lists appear as List-cards (with preview, CTA to Save/Follow).

Profile Page: In “Lists” tab immediately.

Discovery Feed: Shown under “For You” & “Trending” sorted by Circle Score.

2. Food Moment Quick Post
As Taylor, I want to snap/upload a photo, add a caption, and share instantly—so my friends see my meal in their feeds.

Propagation & UI:

Home Feed:

Renders as an Instagram-style card: full-width image, caption overlay, poster avatar, timestamp.

Follow-only feed: users see moments from people they follow or in their Circles.

Engagement CTAs: ❤️ (react), 💬 (comment), 🔖 (save to List) aligned in the thumb-zone.

Profile Page:

Adds a “Moments” tab (or merge with “Posts”) that shows a Pinterest-grid of photos.

Tapping a cell expands into the full-card view.

Discovery Page:

Under “For You” and “Trending” tabs, interleave Food Moments with Lists and Ratings.

Use masonry layout for a dynamic visual flow (Pinterest inspiration).

Follow Alignment:

Only moments from users you follow or Circle members appear—just like Instagram.

When viewing a user’s profile, a “Follow” button sits sticky near the top.
