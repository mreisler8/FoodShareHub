✨ 3–5 UX Principles We Should Adopt
Hero Personalization + Editorial Blends
Combine "For You" + "Trending" hero carousel featuring user‑curated lists, community picks, and location‑based favorites.

Card-Based Modular Feed
Use vertical scroll of clear content types: Curated List, User Post, Trending Spot—each with prominent Save/Follow actions.

Clear Primary CTAs in Thumb Zone
Place “Save List,” “Follow Creator,” or “Bookmark” beneath content cards; use high‑contrast ghost buttons with transitions.

Placeholder-Driven Onboarding
For new users or empty feed, show prompts: “Follow travelers or cuisines” + miniature previews of curated lists.

Embrace Micro‑Interactions & Accessibility
Animated buttons, live previews, aria-live announcements; all touch zones ≥48px; clear focus states and contrast.
